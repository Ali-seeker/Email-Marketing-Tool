# Zero-Budget Cold Email Outreach Plan

Oct 9, 2026 · @Ali Husnain

## Overview

Yeh plan aapko bina ek rupya kharch kiye 30 din mein pehli cold email campaign tak le jata hai; steps ko isi order mein follow karein, kyunke har step agle ki buniyad hai.

**Goal (30 din):** 100 se 120 targeted emails bhej kar 3 se 6 replies aur 1 se 2 discovery calls hasil karna (hafta 1 se 3 tayyari aur warm-up hai, asli outreach hafta 4 se shuru hoti hai).

**Is plan ke 4 rules**

- **Quality > quantity:** 100 sahi logon ko likhi hui personal email, 1,000 generic emails se behtar hai.
- **Chhoti scale:** free Gmail pe roz 20 se 30 emails se zyada nahi, warna spam ka khatra barh jata hai.
- **Pehle warm-up, phir outreach:** naye ya kam use hone wale account se achanak bulk mail spam mein jati hai.
- **Har step ke end pe "Done when"** check hai; wo poora na ho to agle step pe na jayein.

**Steps ka order**

1. Niche aur ideal client chunein
2. Offer, portfolio aur LinkedIn ready karein
3. Free email setup aur warm-up (2 se 3 hafte)
4. Free mein leads jama karein
5. Emails dhoondein aur verify karein
6. Cold email likhein
7. Chhote batches mein safely bhejein
8. Follow-up sequence chalayein
9. Replies handle karein aur sheet mein track karein
10. Daily aur weekly routine banayein

**Realistic expectation:** cold email ka reply rate aam taur par 2 se 5% hota hai, yaani 100 emails pe 2 se 5 replies. Pehli campaign seekhne ke liye hai; asal results 2 se 3 campaigns ke baad aate hain, jab subject, message aur niche tweak ho chuke hon.

**Zero-budget ki hadd:** free Gmail se bhejne ka matlab hai ke aap custom domain se nahi bhej rahe, is liye volume kam rakhna zaroori hai. Pehla client milte hi domain aur paid tools ki taraf jayein (aakhri section dekhein).

## Step 1: Niche aur ideal client (ICP) chunein

Sabse pehle ek hi tarah ke client ko target karein, kyunke ek niche ko likhi hui email zyada relevant lagti hai aur reply rate seedha barhta hai. "Har business ko software chahiye" jaisa soch ke bhejne se reply nahi aate.

**Kaise karein (30 se 45 minute)**

1. Apni 1 se 2 strongest services likhein (jaise web development, AI automation, MVP building).
2. Un businesses ki list banayein jinhein in services ki sabse zyada zaroorat hai.
3. Neeche wali table apni ek niche ke liye bhar lein.

| Factor | Example | Aap ka jawab |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Industry | E-commerce stores, clinics, real estate agencies, startups |  |
| Company size | 5 se 50 employees (decision maker tak pohanch asaan) |  |
| Country | Aisa market jahan budget behtar ho (US, UK, UAE, Australia, Canada) |  |
| Decision maker | Founder, CEO, Marketing Head, Operations Manager |  |
| Unka problem | Purani website, manual kaam, slow support, lead follow-up nahi |  |
| Aap ka solution | Naya site, automation, chatbot, custom dashboard |  |

**Behtar niche chunne ka asaan test:** kya aap iss niche ke 5 existing businesses abhi dhoondh kar unki website mein 1 problem bata sakte hain? Agar haan, to niche sahi hai.

**Done when:** aapke paas 1 niche, 1 country aur 1 decision-maker title likha hua hai.

## Step 2: Offer, portfolio aur online profile ready karein

Email ke baad client sabse pehle aapko Google ya LinkedIn pe check karta hai, isliye email bhejne se pehle ye teen cheezein tayyar honi chahiye; warna achi email bhi trust nahi bana pati.

**A. Ek clear offer likhein (1 paragraph)**

Format: "Main \[niche\] ki \[problem\] solve karta hoon, \[result\] ke saath, \[time\] mein." Misal: "Hum e-commerce stores ke liye order aur support automation banate hain, jis se team ka manual kaam kam hota hai." Isi paragraph se aapki email ka pehla jumla banega.

**B. Free portfolio page**

- GitHub Pages, Netlify ya Vercel pe free single-page site banayein (aap developer hain, ye 1 se 2 din ka kaam hai).
- 2 se 3 projects dikhayein: problem, aap ka solution, tech stack, aur agar ho to result.
- Contact section mein email aur Calendly link rakhein.
- Agar client project nahi hain, to apne personal ya demo projects ko case study ki tarah likhein.

**C. LinkedIn profile**

- Professional photo, headline jaise "Founder at \[Company\] | Web and AI Automation for \[Niche\]".
- About section mein wahi offer paragraph.
- Featured section mein portfolio link.

**D. Professional email signature**

Naam, designation, company, website link. Signature mein 1 se zyada link na rakhein aur images avoid karein (spam score ke liye).

**Done when:** portfolio link live hai, LinkedIn profile complete hai, aur offer ka ek paragraph likha hua hai.

## Step 3: Free email setup aur warm-up (2 se 3 hafte)

Free Gmail se bhejne ka faida ye hai ke SPF, DKIM aur DMARC Google khud sambhalta hai, lekin warm-up ke baghair naya ya kam use hone wala account bulk emails pe jaldi spam mein chala jata hai. Isliye pehle account ki reputation banayein.

**Account setup**

1. Agar aapka purana Gmail aur uski history hai (mahino se use mein), wohi behtar hai; naya account banana ho to professional naam rakhein, jaise `ali.husnain.dev@gmail.com`.
2. Profile photo, poora naam aur 2-Step Verification on karein.
3. Gmail settings mein signature (Step 2 wala) add karein.
4. Outreach ke liye is account ko alag rakhein; personal emails ke liye doosra account use karein.

**Warm-up schedule (sirf real logon ke saath)**

| Hafta | Emails/day | Kis ko bhejein | Kya karna hai |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 se 10 | Dost, classmates, colleagues, developer community | Chhoti natural baat-cheet likhein aur reply maangein |
| 2 | 10 se 15 | Wahi log + community ke naye contacts | Replies ka jawab dein, emails ko star ya important mark karwayein |
| 3 | 15 se 25 | Pehle 10 se 20 cold leads (test batch) | Result dekhein: koi bounce ya spam complaint? |

**Warm-up ke qawaid**

- Sirf asli logon se asli replies lein; fake ya automated warm-up groups se dur rahein.
- Dostoon se kahein: agar email spam mein jaye to "Not spam" karein aur reply zaroor dein.
- Roz thori thori activity rakhein (emails bhejna, reply dena, inbox check karna), ek din 50 aur agle din 0 na bhejein.
- Attachment ya links is dauran na bhejein.

**Done when:** 2 se 3 hafte ho chuke hain, aap 15 se 25 emails/day comfortably bhej rahe hain, aur koi email spam mein nahi gayi.

## Step 4: Free mein leads jama karein

Warm-up ke dauran (hafta 1 se 3) hi leads ki list banate rahein, taake warm-up khatam hote hi bhejne ke liye 100 se 200 tayyar leads hon. Agar aapke paas leads pehle se hain, to unhein neeche wali Google Sheet ke format mein laa kar seedha Step 5 pe jayein.

**Pehle Google Sheet banayein** (naam: "Outreach Tracker") is ke columns:

```csv
Company,Website,Contact Name,Title,Email,Country,Personal Note,Verified,Email 1 Date,Follow-up 1,Follow-up 2,Reply Status
```

**Free lead sources**

| Source | Kaise use karein | Behtar kis niche ke liye |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn search | Title + industry + country filter lagayein, company page aur founder profile dekhein | Startups, agencies, B2B companies |
| Google Maps | "dental clinic Dubai" jaisa search karein, website aur contact copy karein | Local businesses |
| Company websites | "About" aur "Team" page se founder ka naam aur email nikalein | Har niche |
| Apollo.io free plan | Filters laga kar leads export karein (free credits limited hain) | B2B companies |
| Clutch, Product Hunt, Crunchbase (free view) | Nayi funded ya active companies dhoondein | Startups |

**Lead choose karne ke qawaid**

- Sirf wohi lead list mein daalein jis ki website ya LinkedIn dekh kar aap 1 specific baat (problem ya achievement) likh sakte hain; wahi "Personal Note" column mein jayegi.
- Personal ya generic emails (info@, support@) ke bajaye decision maker ka naam wala email pasand karein.
- Aik company se sirf ek contact pe pehle email bhejein.

**Daily target:** 15 se 20 leads roz research karein; 10 din mein 150 se 200 leads ho jayengi.

**Done when:** sheet mein kam az kam 150 leads hain, har ke saath company, naam, title aur 1 personal note maujood hai.

## Step 5: Emails dhoondein aur verify karein

Ghalat ya purani email address pe bheji mail bounce hoti hai, aur bounce rate 2% se upar jaye to Gmail aapke account ko spam ki taraf dhakel deta hai; isliye har email verify karke hi bhejein.

**Email dhoondne ke free tareeqe (is order mein try karein)**

1. Company website ka Contact, About ya Team page.
2. LinkedIn profile ka Contact info section (kabhi kabhi email likha hota hai).
3. Hunter.io ya Snov.io ki free searches (har domain ka email pattern batate hain).
4. Pattern guess karein: `first@company.com`, `first.last@company.com`, `firstinitiallast@company.com`; phir verify karein.

**Verify kaise karein**

1. Hunter.io ya Snov.io ka free email verifier use karein; free credits khatam hon to MillionVerifier jaisi service ke free trial credits check karein.
2. Sirf "valid" status wali emails rakhein; "invalid" hata dein.
3. "Catch-all" aur "risky" emails pehle batch mein na bhejein.
4. Sheet ke "Verified" column mein Yes ya No likhein.

**Kya na karein**

- Kharidi hui ya scrape ki hui bulk lists use na karein; ye spam traps se bhari hoti hain aur account ban karwa sakti hain.
- Bina verify kiye "try karte hain" wali approach na apnayein.

**Target:** bounce rate 2% se kam. 100 emails mein 2 se zyada bounce aaye to foran sending rok kar list dobara check karein.

**Done when:** kam az kam 100 verified emails sheet mein hain.

## Step 6: Cold email likhein

Ek achhi cold email chhoti, personal aur sirf ek sawal ya ek CTA wali hoti hai; lambi "hum ye ye karte hain" wali email na reply laati hai na inbox mein tikti hai. Email client ki zuban (English) mein likhein.

**Email ke qawaid**

- Length: 70 se 120 words, plain text (koi fancy design, banner ya images nahi).
- Pehle jumle mein unki company ke baare mein ek specific baat (Sheet ka "Personal Note").
- Doosre jumle mein aap kaun hain aur kis ki madad karte hain.
- Ek hi CTA: 15 minute ki call ya chhota sawal.
- Pehli email mein 0 se 1 link, koi attachment nahi, koi tracking link ya URL shortener nahi.
- Aakhir mein opt-out line zaroor rakhein.

**Subject line ke options (chhoti aur natural)**

- Quick question about {{Company}}
- {{FirstName}}, idea for {{Company}}
- Question about {{Company}}'s \[website / support / onboarding\]

Subject mein FREE, GUARANTEED, URGENT, `!!!` ya ALL CAPS na likhein.

**Email 1 ka template**

```markdown
Subject: Quick question about {{Company}}

Hi {{FirstName}},

I came across {{Company}} and noticed {{personal note: one specific thing from their site or LinkedIn}}.

I run {{YourCompany}}, a small software team that helps {{niche}} with {{your service}}. Recently we {{one short real example of your work}}.

Would a quick 15-minute call this week make sense to see if we could help {{Company}} in a similar way?

Best,
Ali Husnain
{{YourCompany}} | {{portfolio link}}

P.S. If this isn't relevant, just tell me and I won't email again.
```

**Bhejne se pehle checklist**

- Kya personal note sach aur specific hai?
- Kya email 120 words se chhoti hai?
- Kya sirf ek CTA hai?
- Kya `{{...}}` wale placeholders sab replace ho gaye hain?
- Kya email Mail-Tester.com pe 8/10 ya us se zyada score karti hai? (apna test email check karein)

**Done when:** aapke paas 1 template aur 2 subject lines tayyar hain, aur personal note har lead ke liye sheet mein maujood hai.

## Step 7: Chhote batches mein safely bhejein

Bulk ka matlab free Gmail pe ek saath 200 emails nahi, balke 10 se 15 din mein 150 se 200 emails roz thori thori bhejna hai; yehi tareeqa inbox placement ko bachata hai.

**Pehla test batch (Day 1 se 3)**

1. Sheet se sirf 10 se 15 verified leads chunein.
2. Pehle apne doosre email address pe test email bhej kar dekhein: formatting, placeholders aur spam folder.
3. Phir 10 se 15 emails bhejein aur 2 se 3 din ruk kar bounce aur replies dekhein.
4. Sab theek ho to roz ka volume 20 se 25 tak le jayein.

**Bhejne ke tareeqe**

| Tareeqa | Kaise | Kab behtar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manual Gmail | Template copy karein, personal note daal kar bhejein, "Schedule send" use karein | Shuru ke 50 emails, sab se zyada personal |
| Mail merge add-on (Mailmeteor ya YAMM) | Google Sheet ke columns se name aur note auto-fill hota hai | 50 se zyada leads, free daily limit ke andar |

**Sending ke qawaid (inbox mein rehne ke liye)**

- Roz 20 se 25 emails, 30 se zyada kabhi nahi.
- Mangal se Jumeraat, client ke local time mein subah 9 se 11 baje.
- Har email ek hi person ko; BCC mein bulk kabhi nahi.
- Emails ek ke baad ek minuton ke gap se bhejein, ek saath nahi.
- Open tracking aur click tracking band rakhein; reply hi asli metric hai.
- Har lead ka naam aur personal note badal kar bhejein, template ke alfaaz thore thore vary karein.

**Foran sending rokne ke signs**

| Signal | Kya karein |
| --- | --- |
| Bounce rate 2% se upar | Verification dobara karein, list saaf karein |
| "Message blocked" ya Google ka warning | 3 se 5 din ruk kar sirf warm-up emails bhejein |
| Test email apne inbox mein spam mein jaye | Content badlein (links, words) aur dobara test karein |
| Koi spam complaint ya naraz reply | Us contact ko list se hata dein, sending slow karein |

**Done when:** test batch bina bounce ke nikal gaya hai aur aap roz 20 se 25 emails bhej rahe hain.

## Step 8: Follow-up sequence chalayein

Zyada tar replies pehli email ke baad follow-up pe aate hain, kyunke log busy hote hain aur pehli email bhool jate hain; isliye har lead ko 3 follow-ups tak zaroor bhejein, har baar naye angle ke saath.

| Email | Din | Angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email 1 | Day 1 | Personal note + offer + 15 minute call |
| Follow-up 1 | Day 4 | Chhoti yaad-dehani + ek useful idea ya tip |
| Follow-up 2 | Day 8 | Chhota result ya project example |
| Follow-up 3 (breakup) | Day 14 | Polite aakhri email, bina dabao ke |

**Follow-up ke qawaid**

- Follow-up usi thread mein (Reply ke tor par) bhejein, taake pehli email nazar ke samne rahe.
- "Just checking in" akela na likhein; har baar kuch naya dein.
- Jis lead ne reply kar diya, us ki sequence foran band kar dein aur sheet mein status badal dein.
- Follow-up bhi 50 se 80 words ke andar rakhein.

**Follow-up templates**

```markdown
Follow-up 1 (Day 4)

Hi {{FirstName}},

Quick follow-up on my last note. One idea for {{Company}}: {{one specific, useful suggestion related to their site or process}}.

Happy to share how we would set it up if you are open to a short call.

Best,
Ali
```

```markdown
Follow-up 2 (Day 8)

Hi {{FirstName}},

We recently {{short real example of a project and what it improved}}. It looked similar to what {{Company}} may be dealing with.

Would it be useful if I sent a short summary of how it worked?

Best,
Ali
```

```markdown
Follow-up 3 (Day 14, breakup)

Hi {{FirstName}},

I will stop emailing so I do not clutter your inbox. If improving {{area}} becomes a priority later, feel free to reach out anytime.

Wishing {{Company}} the best,
Ali
```

**Done when:** teeno follow-ups sheet ke Follow-up 1 aur 2 columns ki dates ke saath schedule ho chuke hain.

## Step 9: Replies handle karein aur sab kuch track karein

Reply aate hi 1 se 2 ghante mein jawab dein, kyunke jitni jaldi jawab dete hain, call book hone ka chance utna barhta hai; aur har lead ka status sheet mein update karte rahein.

**Reply ke hisab se jawab**

| Reply ki qism | Kya karein |
| --- | --- |
| "Interested, let's talk" | Calendly link bhejein aur 2 time slots offer karein |
| "Send more info" | 3 se 4 lines mein summary + portfolio link bhejein, phir call ka sawal poochein |
| "Not now, maybe later" | Shukriya likhein, sheet mein 60 se 90 din baad ki follow-up date daalein |
| "Not interested" | Polite shukriya, aur us ko list se hata dein |
| "Wrong person" | Sahi person ka naam ya email poochein |
| Unsubscribe ya naraz reply | Foran maafi maang kar hamesha ke liye list se hata dein |

**Positive reply ka template**

```markdown
Hi {{FirstName}},

Thanks for getting back to me. Here is my calendar: {{Calendly link}}. If none of the slots work, tell me what suits you and I will adjust.

Before the call, could you share one line on what you would like to improve most in {{area}}? It helps me come prepared.

Best,
Ali
```

**Sheet ke Reply Status column ki values:** New, Contacted, Follow-up 1, Follow-up 2, Replied, Interested, Call booked, Not now, Not interested, Bounced.

**Har hafte ye 4 numbers note karein**

- Kitni emails bheji
- Bounce (kitne %)
- Reply (kitne %)
- Positive reply aur booked calls

Agar reply rate 2% se kam hai to subject ya personal note badlein; agar bounce 2% se zyada hai to list verify karein.

**Discovery call se pehle:** client ki website dekhein, 3 sawal likhein (problem, budget range, timeline), aur ek chhota proposal ka outline tayyar rakhein.

**Done when:** har lead ka status updated hai aur har reply ka jawab 24 ghante ke andar gaya hai.

## Spam se bachne ki checklist

Koi bhi tareeqa 100% inbox ki guarantee nahi deta, lekin neeche ki cheezein follow karne se zyada tar emails inbox mein pohanchti hain.

**Email spam mein kyun jati hai**

| Wajah | Is se kaise bachein |
| --- | --- |
| Warm-up ke baghair achanak zyada emails | 2 se 3 hafte warm-up, phir roz 20 se 25 |
| Bounce rate 2% se zyada | Har email verify karein |
| Spam complaints (log "Report spam" dabate hain) | Relevant niche, opt-out line, naraz logon ko foran hata dein |
| Spammy words (FREE, GUARANTEED, URGENT, `!!!`) | Natural, seedhi zuban |
| Zyada links, images, attachments | Pehli email mein 0 se 1 link, plain text |
| Tracking pixels aur URL shorteners (bit.ly) | Tracking band, seedha link |
| Har kisi ko same copy-paste email | Personal note aur thori variation |
| Koi reply ya engagement nahi | Targeted list, achha subject, replies ka jawab dena |
| Kharidi ya scrape ki hui list (spam traps) | Khud research ki hui verified list |
| Unsubscribe ya identity clear nahi | Aakhir mein opt-out line aur poora naam |

**Roz ki 5 aadatein**

1. Bhejne se pehle test email apne doosre inbox pe bhejein.
2. Roz 25 se zyada nahi.
3. Reply aate hi us ki sequence band karein.
4. Bounce aur complaints har hafte ginein.
5. Spam folder mein gayi email ko "Not spam" mark karein (apne test accounts pe).

**Agar emails spam mein jane lagein**

1. 3 se 5 din ke liye cold sending band karein, sirf warm-up emails (real logon ko) bhejein.
2. Apni email ko Mail-Tester.com pe check karein aur jo words ya links flag hon, hatayein.
3. List dobara verify karein aur bounce hone wali emails nikaalein.
4. Daily volume aadha kar ke dobara shuru karein.
5. Behtar hai ke jaldi hi custom domain aur dedicated mailbox ki taraf jayein (agle section mein).

## Legal basics aur paid tools par kab jayein

B2B cold email aam taur par jaiz hai, lekin har market ke apne rules hain, isliye shuru se hi in basics ko follow karein (ye legal advice nahi hai; badi campaigns se pehle apne target country ke rules khud verify karein).

**Legal basics**

- **Poori identity:** apna asli naam, company aur contact details email mein rakhein; misleading subject line na likhein.
- **Opt-out:** har email mein sidhi opt-out line rakhein aur kisi ke mana karne par us ko dobara email na karein.
- **US (CAN-SPAM):** sender ki asli pehchan aur opt-out zaroori hai.
- **UK aur EU (GDPR):** B2B mein "legitimate interest" par bheja ja sakta hai, lekin opt-out ka dhyan rakhna aur data kam se kam rakhna zaroori hai.
- **Canada (CASL):** kaafi sakht hai; pehle apni targeting aur consent ke rules check kar lein.

**Jab revenue aane lage, is order mein upgrade karein**

| Kab | Kya upgrade karein | Approx. kharch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pehla client mil jaye | Custom domain khareedein aur Google Workspace mailbox (apne naam wala professional email) | Domain \~$10 se 15/saal, mailbox \~$6 se 7/mahina |
| 2 se 3 clients ya consistent replies | Alag outreach domain (2 domains, har ke 2 mailboxes) taake main domain mehfooz rahe | Domain + mailbox ki laagat |
| Volume 30 se zyada emails/day chahiye | Sending tool (Instantly ya Smartlead) jis mein warm-up aur rotation hota hai | \~$30 se 100/mahina |
| Leads ki research mein bohat waqt lag raha ho | Apollo ka paid plan aur email verification tool | \~$50 se 100/mahina |

Paid tools ki tabhi zaroorat hai jab free setup se aapko replies aur calls aane lagein; pehle process aur message sahi karein, phir tools badhayein.
